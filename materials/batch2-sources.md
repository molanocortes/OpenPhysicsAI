## Along and across the layers (GOALS.md G17 step 2, 2026-10-07)

Nine records carry Young's modulus and tensile strength along the layers and across them (`youngs_modulus_along_layers_pa`,
`youngs_modulus_across_layers_pa`, `tensile_strength_along_layers_pa`, `tensile_strength_across_layers_pa`), each pair from
one document, which `tools/matcheck.py` checks. The five Polymaker pages above were read again on 2026-10-07 through
their HTML, and every value matched the batch-2 reading; the PolyLite PLA page is new. The fingerprint is the SHA-256 of
the page's mechanical table as text (tags replaced by `|`), because the pages themselves change with every load. The
PA6-CF20, Nylon 12 and ULTEM 9085 values are batch 2's reading of the PDFs listed above (2026-09-19), not downloaded again.

Read as follows. Polymaker's X-Y specimens are printed flat and its Z specimens upright; Stratasys' XZ specimens are
printed on edge, with the layers still along the specimen, and its ZX specimens upright. Prusament's PLA sheet (above)
gives "horizontal" and "vertical xz", both along the layers, so it gives no across-layer value; the PLA pair comes from
PolyLite PLA. The tensile strength is the largest stress of the test: for Nylon 12 and ULTEM 9085 along the layers that
is the yield, as those specimens neck and break lower. The ABS sheet prints a second row labelled X-Y with the value of its
Z row; it is read as X-Y 33.4 MPa (the first row) and Z 29.7 MPa.
